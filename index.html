01 / About

About me

I'm an M.Tech student in Electric Vehicles at IIT Madras, with a B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from VNIT Nagpur. So far my path has mixed academic study with hands-on industry work.

After VNIT, I worked as a Graduate Engineer Trainee at Jindal Stainless on electrical and instrumentation maintenance, industrial projects, equipment upgrades and plant engineering. That time showed me how electrical and control systems behave in real industrial environments.

At IIT Madras I'm building deeper expertise in EV technology. This semester I designed a 3.6 kW three-port SiC DC/DC converter for an EV auxiliary power module, modelled charging and cell balancing of an LFP battery pack from lab data, and built closed-loop DC motor speed control on an STM32. I also serve as Deputy Placement Coordinator for my department.

DC/DC Converter

Three-Port SiC DC/DC Converter for EV Auxiliary Power

ED5023 Power Converter Design · Jul–Nov 2026

Design of a 3.6 kW dual-active-bridge converter that supplies both the 48 V and 12 V buses of an EV from the high-voltage traction battery, using SiC MOSFETs, with simulation in PLECS and the PCB in Altium.

Report · GitHub
Cell Balancing

LFP Battery Pack Charging & Cell Balancing

ED5361 Energy Storage & Conversion · Jul–Nov 2026

Comparison of charging methods and passive vs active cell balancing on a four-cell LFP battery pack, using a Simscape cell model built from lab test data, including the effect on efficiency and battery ageing.

Report · GitHub

ED5211 E-Mobility Lab · Jul–Nov 2026

Testing a 12 V DC geared motor and building closed-loop speed control on an STM32 using MATLAB/Simulink, with feedforward compensation and sensorless speed estimation.
