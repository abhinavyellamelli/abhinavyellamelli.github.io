About Me

I’m Abhinav Yellamelli, an M.Tech student in Electric Vehicles at IIT Madras, with a background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from VNIT Nagpur. My journey so far has given me a mix of academic knowledge and practical industry experience.

After graduating from VNIT, I worked as a Graduate Engineer Trainee at Jindal Stainless, where I was involved in electrical and instrumentation maintenance, industrial projects, equipment upgrades, and plant engineering activities. This experience helped me understand how electrical and control systems work in real industrial environments.

I’m now pursuing my M.Tech at IIT Madras to build deeper expertise in electric vehicle technologies. Alongside my coursework, I enjoy working on technical projects, experimenting with simulations and embedded systems, and continuously learning new technologies. I’m particularly interested in turning the concepts I learn into practical engineering solutions.

Research Interests

  • Battery Management Systems (BMS) — SOC/SOH estimation, cell balancing, battery modelling, and energy management.
  • Automotive Embedded Systems — microcontrollers, embedded software, real-time systems, and automotive electronics.
  • EV Powertrains — electric machines, motor drives, powertrain control, and energy efficiency.
  • Power Electronics — converters, inverters, motor-drive systems, and digital control.
  • Intelligent Control — control strategies and data-driven approaches for improving EV system performance and reliability.
